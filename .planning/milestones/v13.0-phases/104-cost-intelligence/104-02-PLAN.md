## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-104-05 | Spoofing | new `gateway_task_completed` case in `convex/runtimeIngest.ts` | mitigate | No new route and no second auth check — the case sits inside the same `switch` already behind `validateIngestAuth`'s Bearer gate.
