<objective>
Prove the `inbox` janitor's guarantees — and prove each one with a shape matched to its actual
strength.

Purpose: the two safety guarantees in this phase have DIFFERENT strengths, and testing them the
same way would look thorough while proving half of what it claims. The absent-`closedAt`
exclusion is STRUCTURAL: the index range never returns those rows, and that survives an edit
deleting every post-query predicate in the file. The `held`/`money` carve-outs are ORDINARY
POST-QUERY PREDICATES with no database-level backstop — nothing fails when they are removed
except a test written to catch it.

Output: new describe blocks in `convex/inbox.test.ts` covering Verifications A, B, C, D, the
R-02 never-patches-`ackedAt` regression, and a unit-scale control.
</objective>
