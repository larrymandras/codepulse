<objective>
Build `internal.ideation.autoCloseAndPrune`: the same bounded two-step chain as the `inbox`
janitor, keyed on `ideationFindings`' existing `dismissed`/`dismissedAt` fields.

Purpose: `ideationFindings` is a 470-row triage queue growing at 5.1 rows/day in which nobody
has ever dismissed anything — 0 of 470 rows are dismissed. A `dismissedAt`-keyed janitor alone
would delete exactly zero rows forever: an inert mechanism that looks like a fix. Auto-dismiss
at 180 days is what gives the delete step anything to act on.

Output: constants, two step predicates, one exported handler, one `internalMutation` wrapper —
in `convex/ideation.ts`, beside that table's other mutations (D-01).

**This janitor is inert by design until roughly 2026-11-16.** The oldest row was 94 days old on
2026-08-21 and M is 180 days (R-01 rejected shortening it). For about 83 more days it will match
zero rows every night. The mandatory log line is the ONLY thing that will distinguish
correct-and-dormant from dead-on-arrival in that window, which is why R-01 promoted it from a
nicety to a requirement.
</objective>

Every `ideationFindings` timestamp writer uses `Date.now() / 1000` — EPOCH SECONDS:
- `ideation.ts` `recordFinding` → inserts `createdAt: Date.now() / 1000`, `dismissed: false`
- `ideation.ts` `dismissFinding` → patches `{ dismissed: true, dismissedAt: Date.now() / 1000 }`

dismissed-without-a-cursor-value. There are zero dismissed rows today anyway.

`by_dismissed: ["dismissed","createdAt"]`, `by_content_hash`, `by_dismissedAt: ["dismissedAt"]`

`.withIndex("by_dismissed", q => q.eq("dismissed", false)).take(20)` — undismissed, newest 20.
