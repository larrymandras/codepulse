    **1. Live-data smoke (D-01, D-02).**
    Open `http://localhost:5173/workspace-map`. Confirm:
    - The map paints. Count the visible hubs: 1 center + 4 department hubs + the root ring.
    - Click a root hub. Its children appear **immediately** with no visible network wait — expansion
      is client-side over the already-fetched payload.
    - Click the same hub again. It collapses, and its descendants disappear with it.

      number.

    **2. Staleness (D-17).**
    Read the coverage strip's scan-time chip. The scan is registered daily at 04:15. If it reads
    "overdue" with warn styling, that is the strip doing its job and it means the nightly scan did not
    fire — record it, do not dismiss it. Phase 115's D-05 unattended firing was still open at planning
    time, and a staleness indicator is exactly what surfaces a scheduler that silently stopped.

    **3. Theme legibility (Claude's Discretion / UI-SPEC open items).**
    Cycle the ThemeSwitcher through all four dark themes — `cyan`, `emerald`, `readable`, `aubergine`
    — and the light theme. For each, record whether the four department fills plus the muted

      monochrome Paperclip palette. Say whether you want to keep them or revert to
      department-readable-only-in-the-side-panel (a one-line revert: drop the three `:root` values).

    **4. D-18 — Chrome DevTools Issues tab. Observe and record ONLY.**
    With `/workspace-map` open, press F12, go to the **Issues** tab (not Console — it is a separate
    surface that aggregates deprecations, CSP/CORS/cookie findings and breaking-change notices that
    never print to the console, which is why a clean console says nothing about it). Expand whatever
    entry is there and **write down its category and source verbatim** — the exact wording, not a
    paraphrase. If there are several, record all of them. If there are none, record that too, and
    note that this is different from the Phase 111 observation where a `1 Issue` badge was visible
    and never opened.
    **Do not fix anything.** Any fix that falls out is filed against the phase that owns the
    offending code, not built here. That scoping is what keeps an open-ended browser investigation
    out of this phase.

    become the redacted placeholder, while node positions, sizes and colors are visibly unchanged.
