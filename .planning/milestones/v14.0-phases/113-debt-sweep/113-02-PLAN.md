<objective>
Ship the SERVER half of DEBT-05's both-sides guard: teach `computeSkillPrunes` that a producer which
declares its coverage exhaustively may only prune the origins it declared (D-03), make a refused
prune write an `alerts` row (D-05/D-06), and wire both of `convex/registry.ts`'s prune call sites
through one shared helper (D-01).

Purpose: the guard must hold even for producers we do not control. `native` and `bridge` (410 rows,
astridr-side) will keep sending the legacy shape indefinitely, and research could not establish which
astridr code actually emits them. So the rule has to be: an exhaustive declaration RESTRICTS what may
be pruned; the absence of one leaves today's behaviour byte-identical. Anything else either fails to
guard, or permanently freezes 410 rows we have no way to prune — a regression wearing a fix's clothes.

Output: two new pure functions in `convex/skillSync.ts`, one shared refusal-alert writer used by both
mutations in `convex/registry.ts`, and unit coverage including the mandated load-bearing control.
</objective>
