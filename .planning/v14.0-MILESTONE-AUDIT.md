### AUDIT METHOD NOTE — the 3-source matrix would have corrupted MISSION-01

The audit workflow's status-determination matrix says: *VERIFICATION passed + SUMMARY frontmatter
lists the requirement + REQUIREMENTS.md shows `[ ]` → **satisfied**, update the checkbox.*

Applied mechanically to MISSION-01, that rule fires: 111's VERIFICATION is `passed`, and
`111-01-SUMMARY.md` frontmatter reads `requirements-completed: [MISSION-01, MISSION-03]`.

**The checkbox was NOT ticked.** REQUIREMENTS.md:48 records that this exact box has already been
auto-re-ticked twice by two different tools and reverted twice, and states that the traceability
row — not the checkbox — is the authoritative disposition. The matrix, followed blindly,
reproduces a known defect for the third time. It is recorded here so the next auditor does not
"fix" the discrepancy.

Related, and checked this session: `gsd-sdk query phase.complete 118` reported
`requirements_updated: true`, which is the mechanism that re-ticked MISSION-01 once before. Its
diff touched only ROADMAP.md and STATE.md, and both MISSION boxes remain `[ ]`. The hazard did
not fire this time — verified, not assumed.
