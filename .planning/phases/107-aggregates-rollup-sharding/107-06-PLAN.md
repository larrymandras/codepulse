<objective>
Take the after-window measurement and render the OCC-01 verdict.

Purpose: everything before this plan is either a unit test or a deploy. OCC-01 is a live operational requirement — "eliminate OCC write contention on the shared `aggregates` counter rows" — and the only valid evidence is the Convex runtime's own OCC error output in `docker logs`, compared like-for-like against the pre-deploy baseline. An in-code counter cannot substitute: `console.log` inside a UDF does not reach `docker logs` on self-hosted Convex (confirmed 2026-07-30), and the OCC error is emitted by the runtime, not by application code.

This plan is where five specific false-greens are actively refused:
1. Unequal windows before and after.
2. Comparison against the stale `1135/24h` figure instead of the measured baseline rate.
3. A rate drop produced by a quiet period rather than by the fix.
4. A lower OCC count paired with a silently broken fold.
5. A count taken from the unfiltered log rather than scoped to the `aggregates` table.

Output: sections F0, F and G appended to `.planning/phases/107-aggregates-rollup-sharding/107-OCC-EVIDENCE.md`. No source file is modified.
</objective>
