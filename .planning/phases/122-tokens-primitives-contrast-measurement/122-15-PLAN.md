<interfaces>
### The vocabulary these conversions read

`src/lib/metricState.ts` defines the six states with their default copy, icon and tone --
`"no signal yet"` is the fixed empty phrasing (design law) and lives there, not at the call site.
`src/components/EmptyState.tsx` renders that vocabulary at panel/page scale.
`src/components/ui/skeleton.tsx` is the `bg-muted` placeholder idiom for the loading shape.
D-20 permits a per-site copy override wherever genuine context exists ("no runs in the last 24h" is
better than "no signal yet"); the default is what a site falls back to, not a ceiling.

### Design law binding this conversion
Skeletons are shaped like the content they replace -- a table skeleton has rows, a metric skeleton has
a label block and a numeral block, a chart skeleton has the chart's footprint. A generic grey bar is
not a skeleton, it is a different placeholder.

### The two populations, with their units named

- `git grep -hoF '>Loading' -- 'src/*.tsx' | wc -l` -> **58 OCCURRENCES**, but that figure INCLUDES
  test files. CONTEXT.md's D-15 quotes 58 without that qualification. Re-derive both the with-tests
  and without-tests figures and state the difference explicitly rather than adopting either silently.
- em-dash placeholder expressions (`: "—"` or `?? "—"`) -> **52 OCCURRENCES across 28 FILES**
  non-test, of which 12 files are in `src/components/` root and are this plan's. CONTEXT.md's D-15
  quotes 27; that is a different pattern or a different scope. Re-derive, name your regex, and
  reconcile.

Do not quote CONTEXT.md's numbers. They are prior measurements to be re-derived, and this phase's
planning has already found three of its inherited counts to be measuring something other than what
they were quoted as.

### What is NOT a placeholder
An em dash is legitimate typography in a range (`10—20`), as a unit or dash in prose, or in a label.
Only an em dash rendered WHERE A VALUE WOULD GO is the defect. Read each site. Converting a legitimate
dash to `"no signal yet"` is a new false claim, not a fix.

### Files whose colour work belongs to earlier plans
Every file in this slice was already swept for colour by plans 122-04, 122-06 or 122-08 in wave 3.
This plan changes only state and copy. Do not re-open colour work.

`src/components/LoadingState.tsx` is the repo's shared loading surface. Whatever it becomes here is
what many of the other sites should route through -- convert it FIRST and let the rest follow it,
rather than converting eight sites eight different ways.
