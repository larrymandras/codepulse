<objective>
Build the three Galdr UI components: the variable fill-in dialog that both Copy and
Send-to-Chat must pass through, the three-target send split-button, and the editor drawer.

Purpose: `Chat.tsx`'s autoSend handoff fires immediately on arrival behind a `firedRef`
guard, with no confirmation step in Chat itself — so an unresolved body handed to it would be
answered by Ástríðr before anyone could correct it. That is why resolution is a blocking step
on the Galdr side, and why D-11 and D-12 are one component, not two.
Output: three components under `src/components/galdr/`, plus tests for the two that carry a
decision.
</objective>
