<objective>
This phase makes a completeness claim about a sweep. "Clean in `src/`" provably does not equal
"clean in what ships": `scripts/migrate_tokens.py` holds Tailwind class names as Python dict keys,
Tailwind's default whole-repo scan reads them, and `.bg-gray-950\/50{background-color:#03071280}`
was still in the built stylesheet after Phase 122 swept every instance out of `src/` and
`index.html`. Either the claim is true or it is explicitly bounded. This plan does both — it
narrows the scan root, and it writes down the two things the ratchet still cannot see.

Purpose: D-07, D-08 and D-17.
Output: an `@source not` exclusion in `src/index.css`, plus `123-SWEEP-BOUNDARY.md` recording the
measured boundary and closing the two folded todos.
</objective>
