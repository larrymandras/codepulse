<objective>
Replace the two destructive confirms that do not live in a dialog: the 5-second auto-dismissing toast
in `Tasks.tsx` and the browser-native `window.confirm` in `WarRoom.tsx`. Both become shadcn
`AlertDialog`s with no timeout.

Purpose: POLISH-03's rule is that a destructive action is confirmed in a dialog, never a toast — a
toast can be missed or auto-dismissed, so it cannot carry a decision. `window.confirm` fails the same
rule from the other direction: it is unthemeable, ignores all five `data-theme` blocks, and on the
more destructive of the two actions (a war room *and its transcript*).

Output: two small controlled `AlertDialog` components, both call sites rewritten to a
pending-state-then-dialog shape, and a test each whose load-bearing assertion is that **Cancel does
nothing** — not merely that the dialog closes.

**Read the premise correction before you start.** ROADMAP success criterion 3 calls
`Tasks.tsx:144-145` a task-**delete** confirm. It is not. It is a **move-to-action-column** confirm
that dispatches a WS command to Ástríðr. It is genuinely the defect POLISH-03 targets, but there is
no task-delete toast anywhere in the app and you must not go looking for one.
</objective>
