<decided_shapes>
Naming, columns, indexes and windows are Claude's Discretion per CONTEXT.md. They are DECIDED
here - the executor implements these, it does not re-choose them.

**Table `governorDecisions`** - fields `emitter` (v.string), `priority` (v.string),
`spoke` (v.boolean), `heldReason` (v.optional(v.string)), `timestamp` (v.float64, epoch seconds).
Single index `by_timestamp` on `["timestamp"]`.
- `emitter` and `priority` stay `v.string()`, never a `v.literal` union. Measured 2026-08-12:
  13 distinct emitters across 1,168 rows, most of them (`cron:task_delivery`, `startup_test`,
  `cron_failure`, `focus_exit_digest`, `watch_pulse_duplicate_alert`, and every
  `cron:<taskname>`) absent from the contract's illustrative list. This matches the schema's own
  defensive-boundary convention documented on `controlVerbSwaps`' `verb`/`path`.
- No `by_profileId` / `by_scope` index: `governor_decision` carries no profile field at all.
  Do not add an index with nothing to key on.

**Table `messageRoutes`** - fields `channel` (v.string), `profile` (v.string),
`sender` (v.optional(v.string)), `sessionId` (v.optional(v.string)), `timestamp` (v.float64,
epoch seconds). Indexes `by_timestamp` on `["timestamp"]` and `by_profile` on
`["profile", "timestamp"]`.
- `sender` and `sessionId` are OPTIONAL even though contract section 2.16 lists them as required.
  Reason: a required field that arrives as an explicit JSON `null` makes the resolver refuse the
  whole event and the row is dropped silently - that is precisely the TELE-02 defect (Phase 108,
  `control_verb_swap`, zero rows ever landed). Storing a partial row is strictly better than
  losing it silently. `channel` and `profile` stay required.
- `by_profile` exists because `profile` is a real field on this kind (unlike
  `governor_decision`) and a future per-profile inbound view is the obvious consumer. If the
  executor judges the unconsumed index not worth carrying, keep it anyway and copy
  `controlVerbSwaps`' own precedent comment style for a deliberately-unconsumed index
  (`schema.ts:2161-2171`).

**Retention windows** (both within the established precedent tiers - RESEARCH.md Secondary Q4):
- `governorDecisions: 30` - matching `controlVerbSwaps`, its closest read-pattern analog
  (append-only audit trail read via a bounded `.take()`, not a latest-per-key lookup). At the
  measured 83.3 rows/day this accumulates roughly 2,500 rows.
- `messageRoutes: 90` - matching `toolPolicyEvents`' rare-event tier. At the measured 0.7-1.2
  rows/day this accumulates well under 200 rows in total, so the window is pure headroom.
</decided_shapes>
