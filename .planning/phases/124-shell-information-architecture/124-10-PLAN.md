### D-06's required sequence — both branches are specified, neither is a failure

1. The consolidated header is already built (plans 124-07 through 124-09).
2. Measure: the combined min-content width of the three header zones versus the header's own
   available width, at 375px and at 900px.
3. **If it clears with margin** — min-content comfortably under available, not merely
   not-currently-overflowing — adopt `h-12` (48px), fixed, dropping `flex-wrap` and `min-h-14`.
4. **If it does not clear with margin** — keep `min-h-14 flex-wrap gap-y-1`, and say so plainly in
   the phase's closing documentation. Do NOT report 48px as shipped when the wrap is what shipped.
   This is not a fallback to be ashamed of; it is the difference between a number and a result.

Either branch leaves the header's own `overflow` unset and the ancestor `overflow-hidden` at
`DashboardLayout.tsx:517`-ish unchanged. POLISH-06's finding about WHERE the clip happens is
independent of which branch wins.

The long POLISH-06 comment above `<header>` (`DashboardLayout.tsx:529-550`) must be **UPDATED, not
deleted**, whichever branch wins — a future reader needs to know the flex-wrap was deliberate,
control-proven history rather than incidental styling.

### E-Stop's 96px

`EStopButton.tsx` carries `shrink-0` (`:98`) and `whitespace-nowrap` (`:101`) but **no explicit
min-width utility**. Sketch §7 states `min-width: 96px`. The UI-SPEC is explicit that the planner
must verify 96px is actually reached rather than assume those two utilities guarantee it — its
current classes are `px-2 py-1 text-sm` around a 16px icon and the word "E-Stop", which is very
unlikely to reach 96px. Measure first.

### The active rail

jsdom cannot compute pseudo-elements, so plan 124-05's unit test could only assert a class string.
`getComputedStyle(el, '::before')` in a real browser can. This plan is where the 2px rail becomes a
rendered measurement.
