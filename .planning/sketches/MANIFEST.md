# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

"Quiet Control Room" — the converged direction from the 3-model redesign comparison (`html-out/ui-premium-redesign-comparison.html`, verdict approved 2026-08-07). Calm matte graphite surfaces in layers (`--surface-0/1/2` + hairlines), color spent only on state, mono demoted to data, one uppercase eyebrow style. Three hue owners: cyan = the machine (interactive + live-now), violet = Ástríðr the entity, `--status-*` = state (ok decoupled from primary). Two signature layers: GPT-5.6 Sol's **Signal Horizon** on the shell (2px event-carrying line, ambient health at a distance, E-Stop turns it crimson) and Kimi K3's **Pulse ECG hero** on the Dashboard. Claude's serif "voice of Ástríðr" rides as a per-surface trial. Kill list: glitch-text, matrix-bg, CRT-default, per-item nav glow, hover scale, pulse-dot decoration, fabricated data.

## Reference Points

- The `/chat` presence page (in-repo north star — transmission bubbles, luminous rails, `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`)
- Linear / Raycast restraint; NASA MOCR / glass-cockpit instrument panels
- The three model proposals: Borealis Console (Claude), Quiet Reactor (GPT-5.6 Sol), Quiet Control Room (Kimi K3)
